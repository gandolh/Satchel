# Task 17 — Share into Satchel from Android's share sheet

## Context

The owner decided on 2026-10-28 ("Android first, iPhone kept working" in
[decisions.md](../../wiki/decisions.md)): Satchel is used on both phones,
Android is the main one and the one features are tested on, and **Android gets
a Web Share Target** so Satchel appears in its share sheet. The iPhone gets no
share sheet (only a native wrapper could give it one, and that was rejected),
but must keep working: Safari ignores `share_target`, so nothing there may
depend on it.

The main use is dropping a link or a line of text into the Ideas inbox for
Claude to read, without opening Satchel and pasting.

Where things are:

- The manifest is generated by vite-plugin-pwa from `web/vite.config.ts`
  (`manifest: { id, scope, start_url, … }`, all relative to `base`).
- The service worker is `web/src/sw.ts` (injectManifest; it owns the
  navigation fallback).
- The router is `web/src/router.ts`, four routes on the History API, no
  library: chats, a thread at `c/:id`, settings, new chat.
- The composer is `web/src/thread/Composer.tsx`. Messages are text only and
  append-only.

## Files you OWN

- `web/vite.config.ts` (the manifest's `share_target`)
- `web/src/router.ts` and its test (a share route)
- `web/src/thread/Composer.tsx` (taking prefilled text), and a small share
  screen if one is needed
- `web/src/sw.ts`, only if the chosen method needs it
- corpus: this brief's outcome, `corpus/wiki/status.md`, `corpus/log.md`

## Files you must NOT touch

- `server/`: sharing posts through the ordinary send path, nothing new on the
  server.

## What to do

1. Add `share_target` to the manifest with `method: "GET"`, an `action` under
   the app's base (for example `share`), and `params` for `title`, `text` and
   `url`. GET needs no service worker handler; text and links are all Satchel
   takes (text only, no files).
2. A `share` route reads the three params, joins them into one message (the
   text, then the URL on its own line if the text doesn't already contain it;
   the title only when there is no text), and opens the **Ideas inbox** with
   that message in the composer, not yet sent. The user can edit, send, or go
   to another chat. Then the address is replaced with the thread's, so a
   reload doesn't paste it twice.
3. Signed out: keep the shared text through the sign-in and land on step 2
   after it.
4. Over the length limit in `shared/src/limits.ts`: the composer shows the
   usual over-limit state; nothing is cut silently.

## Acceptance

- On Android (Chrome, Satchel installed): sharing a page from Chrome lists
  Satchel; picking it opens the Ideas inbox with the link in the composer;
  Send posts it.
- Sharing selected text from another app does the same with the text.
- Reloading after Send doesn't prefill again.
- On iPhone (not tested on a device): the app installs and works as before;
  the manifest change does nothing there. Say in the outcome how this was
  checked (for example, the share route is never the only way to any screen).
- `npm run typecheck`, `npm run lint`, `npm test` pass; Lighthouse or Chrome's
  Application panel shows the manifest valid with the share target.

## Outcome (2026-10-28)

Shipped in `85b5087`. The manifest has a GET `share_target` at
`<base>share` with `title`, `text` and `url`. `web/src/share/share.ts` does
the work in two hand-offs:

- At start-up (`main.tsx` calls `captureShare()`), the share route's query
  becomes one message by the brief's rule. The message is kept in this
  tab's sessionStorage (memory too, where storage throws) and the query is
  dropped from the address. A password sign-in never leaves the page;
  "Sign in with Ward" comes back to the bare `/share` path (`returnToFor`
  drops queries, and a share can be longer than the 512-character
  `returnTo`), and sessionStorage outlives that round trip. The text never
  goes to the server.
- Signed in, `ShareScreen` takes it, offers it to the Ideas inbox as an
  in-memory draft and replaces the address with the thread's. `ThreadScreen`
  reads the draft into `Composer`'s new `initialText` and clears it, so it
  shows once, unsent. A reload after Send or before it doesn't prefill.

Two calls the brief didn't make. First, a friend has no inbox: they land on
the chat list and the first chat they open gets the text. Second, the
composer isn't focused on arrival, so Android's keyboard doesn't cover the
thread; Send is one tap. Over the limit, the usual counter shows ("4100 /
4000") and Send stays off; nothing is cut. Files touched outside the OWN
list, as wiring only: `layout/AppShell.tsx` renders the share screen,
`thread/ThreadScreen.tsx` passes the draft, `main.tsx` captures. `sw.ts` and
`server/` are untouched: the worker's navigation fallback already answers
`/share`.

How it was checked: typecheck, lint and 554 tests pass (18 new: the message
rule, the stash, the capture, the draft, the route). The built app was run
under `vite preview` against a scratch API (scratch `DATA_DIR`, Ward off,
throwaway users) and driven in headless Chrome by a Playwright script.
Chrome's own manifest parser (DevTools `Page.getAppManifest`) reported no
errors and returned the `share_target`, and `Page.getInstallabilityErrors`
was empty. The share flows passed with the params in the URL: signed out,
then a reload standing in for the Ward round trip, then a password sign-in,
landed in the inbox with `A page` and the link in the composer. Send stored
it once, and a reload didn't prefill. Signed in, a text-only share and
Chrome's usual link-in-`text` share landed in the inbox. A reload before
Send cleared it. The over-limit case and Back to the chat list behaved, and
a friend's share went to the first chat they opened, once. Lighthouse and
the Application panel were not used; the CDP calls above are what Chrome
reads.

iPhone, not tested on a device: Safari ignores `share_target` (an unknown
manifest member), and nothing in the app links to `/share`. The share
route is never the only way to any screen, and `captureShare` does nothing
on any other path. The install, the manifest's other members and the worker
are unchanged.

Owner steps, on the Android phone after the next deploy (no phone here):

1. Open Satchel installed from Chrome (reinstall if the share sheet doesn't
   list it; Chrome re-reads the manifest on its update check).
2. In Chrome, Share → Satchel on any page: the Ideas inbox opens with the
   link in the composer. Send, then reload: nothing comes back.
3. Select text in another app, Share → Satchel: the same with the text.
4. Signed out (or with "Sign in with Ward"), share again: after signing in,
   the text is in the inbox's composer.
